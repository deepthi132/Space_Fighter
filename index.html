How to play
  • ← → move the ship.
  • Space fires the selected weapon.
  • 1, 2, and 3 switch weapons.
  • P or Esc pauses the game.
  • On touch screens, drag on the game area to move and fire.

Built with TypeScript, Canvas 2D, and Vite.
